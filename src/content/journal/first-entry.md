Think of this first entry as a statement of purpose. 

My goal with this space is to share my professional journey and life experiences from a few intersecting perspectives: as a data engineer, as Isaac's father, and as someone who has recently embraced therapy to better navigate the rapid pace of our world.

I have always felt a deep need to write, though historically, I kept those words to myself. However, after navigating a particularly challenging year, I feel ready to start sharing my thoughts, projects, and adventures exactly as they take shape in my mind. 

To whoever is reading this—thank you. If my way of expressing things occasionally feels a bit raw or unfiltered, it is by design. While I certainly want to share my technical work with peers and potential employers, a driving reason for this site is much more personal. I hope that one day, my son can read these entries and discover little fragments of his father's character. 

(I do carry a physical diary with me most of the time to capture the deeply personal moments—a journal meant only for Isaac and me when I am no longer in this place or time. But this digital space is meant to be shared here and now.)

So, without further ado, welcome to the journal. Enjoy the read.

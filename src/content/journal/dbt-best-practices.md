---
title: "Three habits that improved my dbt projects"
description: "Clear layers, tests from day one, and documentation as part of the code."
date: 2026-11-10
tags: ["dbt", "snowflake"]
---

1. Separate `staging`, `intermediate` and `marts`.
2. Add `unique` and `not_null` tests to every key.
3. Document in the same YAML as the model.
